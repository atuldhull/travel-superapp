TravelSuperApp — design strategy

A working blueprint of every page, every flow, and every decision that's still open. Use this as the source of truth when you sit down to redesign the front end.

1 · Vision & promise

What it is: a mobile-first, AI-powered super-app that turns a destination + radius into a full paced trip — places, stays, food, events, transport, weather, safety, language — and stays with you through the trip with live re-planning, photo memory books, and a panic button that fans out to your people.

Plan smarter

Drop a pin + a radius + dates. AI drafts a paced day-by-day plan in seconds. Tweak, vote, share.

Travel safer

One-tap SOS to trusted contacts via SMS + push. Crime + scam layer. Country primer for plugs, visa, currency, e-sim.

Remember it

Drop photos as you go. They auto-organise into themed memory books you can publish or keep private.

Suggestion
Pick one of those three as the "lead promise" on the landing page hero. Currently the landing tries to sell all three at once which dilutes the pitch. My recommendation: Plan smarter as the hero, with Safety + Memory as supporting pillars below.

2 · Who uses it

Three primary actors — design every screen with the right one in mind.

🧳 Travelercore

Plans + takes a trip. Casual to power user. Uses 95% of the surface area.

What they care about

  • Time to first plan (< 30 sec)
  • Plans that actually work day-of
  • Photos that come out as memories, not a folder of JPEGs
  • Feeling safe in unfamiliar places
🎫 Local agentdefer to v2

Verified local who bids on Premium-tier trips that need a human (visa-heavy, group, accessibility).

Decision

Agent surface exists in code (3 pages) but feels like marketplace v2. Suggest cutting from main nav and putting behind a "Become a guide" footer link until you have demand.

⚙️ Admin / SRErequired

Internal-only. Moderates content, reviews ban appeals, watches dashboards.

Design tradeoff

Spend the minimum visual effort here — it's internal. Functional > pretty. Same admin shell, dense data tables.

Decision needed
Should Local agent be in the main product or deferred to v2? My recommendation: defer. Concierge feature can ship as "talk to a real planner" Premium perk that emails our team — no marketplace required. Saves 3 pages + 1 onboarding flow.

3 · The 5 core jobs to be done

Every page in the app should serve at least one of these. If a page doesn't, ask: cut it.

J1 Plan a trip — turn a destination into an itinerary /trips/new · /trips/[id] · /trips/[id]/overview Often (start of every trip) keep, polish
J2 Use the plan day-of — open phone, follow the day /trips/[id] · /near-me · /eateries/[id] · /trips/[id]/food-crawl Multiple times per day during trip rethink as mobile
J3 Capture & remember — photos, captions, story /memory-books · /memory-books/[id]/edit During + after trip keep, simplify edit
J4 Stay safe — SOS, scam awareness, country primer SOS FAB (global) · /account/trusted-contacts · trip overview Rare but high stakes keep, never compromise

Suggestion
J1 + J2 + J4 are the "must work in v1". J3 is high-emotion and worth full polish. J5 (social/sharing) can ship as "share via link" only — drop hearts, votes, karma, public profiles for v1. Makes the social surface ~5x simpler.

5 · Journey — first-time visitor → first trip

The single most important flow. Every percentage point of friction here costs you a user. Target: under 90 seconds from landing to seeing a real AI plan.

First-time visitor flow
  1. Lands on / Hero pitch · Sample trip demo (works WITHOUT signup) · Trust strip · Pricing CTA
  2. Tries the sample Pick a city pill ("Goa", "Tokyo") → "Generate 3-day plan" → AI prose appears in ~5 sec → "Save this trip" CTA appears
  3. Hits the conversion wall Save → "Sign up to save your plans" modal → email + password + Google OAuth + magic link
  4. Confirms email (if magic-link) Resend delivers · /auth/magic-link/[token] auto-logs in
  5. 3-step onboarding wizard Interests (food / culture / nature / nightlife) · Comfort mode (default / family / accessible / budget / nomad) · "Start your first trip"
  6. Lands on /trips/new with the sample pre-filled Tweak, save, get the AI plan they already saw, now persisted
  7. Lands on /trips/[id] Empty state with clear CTAs: "Add photos · Find places · Share · Generate plan"
Suggestion
The current sample-trip-demo on the landing page works without signup. Lean into that. Make it the hero interaction — not just a value pillar below the fold. Try-before-you-buy converts way better than "Sign up to start".
Decision needed
Onboarding wizard — keep, simplify, or kill?
  • Keep all 3 steps: better personalization, more friction
  • Simplify to 1 step: just comfort mode (highest signal)
  • Kill it entirely: straight to /trips/new with smart defaults
My pick: Simplify to 1 step + a "later" link.

6 · Journey — returning user → continue trip

Returning visitor (gap > 30 days OR < 30 days)
  1. Lands on / Welcome-back banner appears (V.UX.30) if last seen > 30 days ago. References cached last sample plan if < 24 h.
  2. Auto-redirects to /trips If cookie still has refresh token. Otherwise lands on /login (auto-fills email).
  3. /trips landing Hero: in-progress trip card · "Plan something new" suggestions strip (curated based on history) · Active tab + Archived tab
  4. Continues editing One-tap into the most-recent draft → resume where left off
Suggestion — /trips redesign
Currently /trips is a flat grid of cards. Reshape it as a three-region layout:
  1. Hero: "Active trip" card if one is in-progress (today's date inside trip dates)
  2. Suggestions strip: 3 curated city cards based on recent activity
  3. Archive: collapsed list, "Show 5 more" pattern

7 · Journey — Free → Premium upgrade

Premium upgrade flow (Stripe TEST or LIVE)
  1. Hits a Premium gate Tries "Match a concierge" on /trips/[id]/concierge OR clicks "Upgrade" anywhere → routes to /pricing
  2. /pricing page 3 tiers (Free / Premium $9/mo / Agent contact). "Upgrade to Premium" CTA
  3. POST /payments/checkout Server creates Stripe Checkout session → redirect to checkout.stripe.com
  4. Pays with test card 4242 4242 4242 4242 / any future expiry / any CVC
  5. Stripe redirects → /account/billing Shows "Premium · Active · renews on …"
  6. Webhook flips role checkout.session.completed → SyncSubscription → User.role = 'premium'
  7. Re-login required (one-time) JWT carries role; new role lands on next refresh cycle. Surface a "Sign out + back in to unlock" toast
Decision needed — what's gated as Premium?
Currently gated:
  • /trips/[id]/concierge — agent matching
  • Hand-picked stays + curated-only place filters
  • Premium AI providers (Claude/Gemini vs stub)
  • 50 GB photo storage (vs 1 GB free)
Suggestions to add:
  • Unlimited memory books (free = 3) — high-value, low-cost gate
  • Custom themes / branded sharing — vanity gate
  • Offline pack generation — operationally cheap, perceived high value

8 · Journey — Plan → memory book → share

End-of-trip flow
  1. Returns from trip App detects trip endsOn < today via the auto-archive scheduler
  2. "Make a memory book?" prompt on /trips/[id] Auto-creates a draft book with all photos uploaded during trip dates
  3. /memory-books/[id]/edit Drag-reorder, captions auto-suggested from EXIF + place data, theme picker
  4. Publish Permalink /memory-books/[id] · optional /featured submission · WhatsApp/email share
  5. Friends view at /memory-books/[id] No-account-needed view · "Plan your own" CTA
Suggestion
The auto-prompt step (#2) doesn't exist yet — currently users have to know to navigate to /memory-books/new. Build the prompt as a persistent banner on /trips/[id] when the trip is in the past + has > 5 photos. High-emotion, high-share moment.

9 · Journey — Safety / SOS in the wild

SOS trigger (the most important UX in the app)
  1. Setup (once, before trip) /account/trusted-contacts → add up to 3 numbers + emails. Verify via test ping.
  2. In trouble — taps red SOS FAB Bottom-right, persistent on every page. Always > 56px touch target.
  3. Big confirm modal "HOLD 3 SECONDS" pattern (no accidental triggers). Cancel button huge. Dark theme.
  4. POST /safety/sos Sends lat/lng, trigger type, traveler name. Promise.allSettled fan-out.
  5. SMS lands at all 3 contacts (if Twilio wired) "🆘 SOS from Atul: manual at 38.72,-9.14. Cancel link if false alarm: …"
  6. Persistent banner on every page "SOS active — last sent 2 min ago" with Cancel button. Doesn't go away until resolved.
  7. User cancels OR admin resolves Banner disappears. Audit log entry written. Optional follow-up SMS to contacts: "All clear."
Critical UX rule
SOS must work even if the user has 1 bar of signal, the screen is wet, or they're shaking. Test specifically: dark mode, glove touch, lock-screen access, low battery.
Decision needed
Lock-screen SOS shortcut? iOS/Android both support PWA shortcuts. Worth wiring as v1.5.

24 · Design system summary

  • Sans: Inter (next/font/google) — already wired
  • Mono: ui-monospace, Menlo, Consolas (system stack — fast)
